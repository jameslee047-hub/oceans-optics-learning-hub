# Content Gaps

This document separates gaps evident from the recovered guide from possible future expansion. It does not write the missing content.

## Gaps Evident From The Existing Guide

### Missing referenced planning files

`REVIEW-FLAGS.md` and `EXTRACTION-SUMMARY.md` were referenced in the brief but were not present in `extracted-content`. The available extraction issues and content inventory were used instead.

### Runtime/export issues

The extracted guide notes missing local runtime assets: `loader-default.mp4` and `static/js/main.947f107a.js`. This affects running the old offline export, not the recovered text IA.

### Audio is not educationally mapped

The local MPGA file is referenced by raw audio actions and hidden HTML audio preload markup, but it is not recoverably mapped to a specific lesson source. Manual listening is needed before deciding whether it belongs in the hub.

### Some subjects are only labels plus popups

Several main pages are mostly menu-like labels, with the substance in popups. Examples: `017 Physics`, `018 Physiology`, `021 Pre Snorkel Routine`, `022 Entry & Exit`, `023 Snorkels & Duck Diving`, `024 Kicking Styles`, and `030 Swimming Techniques`.

### Beach flag meanings are minimal

Many flag popups provide only a short meaning line. This is enough for a quick lookup page but thin for a full lesson unless visuals and context are added later.

### First aid and physiology content needs expert review

CPR, rescue breathing, drowning, jellyfish stings, hypocapnia, hypercapnia, and shallow water blackout are high-stakes topics. The existing guide contains substantial material, but publication should require instructor/medical/legal review.

### Scuba/diving positioning is implied but not deeply supported

The recovered material mentions snorkelling heavily and includes scuba-adjacent concepts such as equalizing, airspaces, buoyancy, and wetsuits. It does not contain enough true scuba course material for a scuba-specific hub category yet.

### Product connection is not explicit

The guide discusses masks, prescription-friendly lens formats, snorkels, fins, wetsuits, and safety devices, but it does not map these to Oceans Optics products or shopping tools.

### Visual meaning requires review

Some educational meaning may be carried by images, icons, SVG buttons, and animations. Text was recovered programmatically, but visual-only meaning still needs review before lesson writing.

### Some labels appear duplicated or misfiled

The old menus include repeated or odd labels, such as repeated "Fins" in Academics/Extra Tips and misspelled source titles such as `Enviroment`, `Attatching`, `Open Heal`, and `Decent`. These should not drive the new IA.

## Potential Future Expansion

### Product decision tools

Add mask selector, prescription mask explainer, lens/skirt comparison, snorkel comparison, and fin selector modules.

### Downloadable PDFs

Strong candidates: pre-snorkel checklist, safety devices checklist, mask prep checklist, beach flag reference, first aid reminder, and warm-up/stretch routine.

### Quizzes and checks

Strong candidates: beach flags, golden rules, water movement, mask/snorkel/fins selection, equalizing basics, marine life identification, and conservation practices.

### Video or step-by-step visuals

Strong candidates: mask clearing, snorkel clearing, duck diving, entry/exit, kicking styles, donning a mask, attaching a snorkel, and CPR/first aid if approved.

### Local conditions and trip planning

Future guides could cover reading forecasts, choosing a site, visibility, tides by location, marine stinger seasons, boat traffic, and local beach hazards.

### Underwater vision education

The existing content supports prescription mask selection indirectly. A future expansion could explain underwater optics, prescription lenses, fit tradeoffs, and when to seek support.

### Customer support journeys

Create customer-facing lessons for "I just bought a mask", "my mask fogs", "my mask leaks", "I wear glasses", "I get water in my snorkel", and "what should I pack?"

### Scuba-specific expansion

If Oceans Optics wants the hub to cover diving as well as snorkelling, future content should add regulator basics, BCDs, dive computers, dive planning, certification boundaries, ascent/descent procedures, and dive-specific medical guidance.
