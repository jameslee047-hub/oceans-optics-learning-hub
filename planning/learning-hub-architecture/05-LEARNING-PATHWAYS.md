# Learning Pathways

These pathways use only subjects supported by the recovered material. A lesson can appear in more than one pathway.

## Pathway 1: First-Time Snorkeller

Purpose: Give a beginner enough context to understand the activity, choose essential gear, and enter the water with basic safety awareness.

Lessons:

1. `L01 What Snorkelling Is and How This Guide Helps`
2. `L02 Health, Readiness and Personal Responsibility`
3. `L03 Golden Rules for Safer Snorkelling`
4. `L05 Choosing a Mask`
5. `L06 Preparing, Fitting and Caring for a Mask`
6. `L07 Choosing and Setting Up a Snorkel`
7. `L10 Surface Safety Devices`
8. `L11 Pre-Snorkel Checklist and Planning`
9. `L12 Beach Flags and Surface Warnings`
10. `L14 Buddy Communication and Awareness`
11. `L15 Entry and Exit Techniques`
12. `L17 Mask Clearing Techniques`

Notes: This is the strongest beginner route based on the recovered guide.

## Pathway 2: Safer Snorkelling

Purpose: Surface the most important risk-awareness lessons in one path.

Lessons:

1. `L02 Health, Readiness and Personal Responsibility`
2. `L03 Golden Rules for Safer Snorkelling`
3. `L10 Surface Safety Devices`
4. `L11 Pre-Snorkel Checklist and Planning`
5. `L12 Beach Flags and Surface Warnings`
6. `L13 Understanding Water Movement`
7. `L14 Buddy Communication and Awareness`
8. `L21 Equalizing Basics`
9. `L22 Heat Transfer, CO2 and Blackout Risks`
10. `L25 First Aid Essentials for Snorkelling`

Notes: The first aid and physiology lessons need manual review before publication.

## Pathway 3: Equipment Essentials

Purpose: Help customers choose, fit, use, and care for core snorkelling gear.

Lessons:

1. `L05 Choosing a Mask`
2. `L06 Preparing, Fitting and Caring for a Mask`
3. `L07 Choosing and Setting Up a Snorkel`
4. `L08 Choosing Fins`
5. `L09 Exposure Protection, Hair and Pre-Snorkel Prep`
6. `L10 Surface Safety Devices`
7. `L17 Mask Clearing Techniques`

Notes: This pathway is the clearest place to add natural internal links to Oceans Optics products and prescription-mask tools later.

## Pathway 4: In-Water Skills Builder

Purpose: Give learners a practical sequence for becoming more comfortable and efficient in the water.

Lessons:

1. `L15 Entry and Exit Techniques`
2. `L16 Snorkel Clearing and Duck Diving`
3. `L17 Mask Clearing Techniques`
4. `L18 Efficient Kicking Techniques`
5. `L19 Swimming, Breathing and Floating Skills`
6. `L20 Buoyancy, Airspaces, Light and Sound`
7. `L21 Equalizing Basics`

Notes: This path should eventually benefit from video or image demonstrations, but the lesson topics are supported by the recovered content.

## Pathway 5: Ocean-Aware Snorkeller

Purpose: Help learners understand the ocean environment, what they may see, and how to behave responsibly.

Lessons:

1. `L04 Types of Snorkelling Experiences`
2. `L13 Understanding Water Movement`
3. `L20 Buoyancy, Airspaces, Light and Sound`
4. `L23 Marine Life Basics`
5. `L24 Coral, Conservation and Responsible Behaviour`

Notes: This path is useful for prospective customers who are motivated by exploration and stewardship rather than gear alone.

## Pathway Count

- Proposed pathways: 5.
- Strongest immediate pathways: First-Time Snorkeller, Safer Snorkelling, Equipment Essentials.
- Pathways needing most manual review before launch: Safer Snorkelling, because first aid and blackout-risk topics are high stakes.
