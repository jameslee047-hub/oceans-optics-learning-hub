# Category Proposal

The new Learning Hub should not mirror the old Genially sections. The proposed top-level navigation uses six categories, each built around what a learner is trying to do.

## Proposed Categories

### 1. Start Here

What belongs here: beginner orientation, what snorkelling involves, readiness, responsibility, golden rules, and broad types of snorkelling.

Why it exists: this gives first-time visitors a low-friction path into the hub and establishes Oceans Optics as a practical instructor voice before asking learners to choose detailed topics.

Existing material feeding it: `001` to `007`, `029`, `031`, `033`, `037`, `043`, `072`, `081`, `102`.

Likely audience: first-time snorkellers, prospective customers, parents/families, cautious learners, and people deciding whether snorkelling is right for them.

Primary orientation: snorkelling, safety, beginner confidence, instructor credibility.

### 2. Gear & Fit

What belongs here: masks, prescription-ready mask considerations, snorkels, fins, exposure suits, surface safety devices, setup, comfort checks, and aftercare.

Why it exists: gear questions are likely high-intent customer questions. This category can naturally support internal links to Oceans Optics products and tools without turning lessons into sales pages.

Existing material feeding it: `008` to `012`, `034`, `036`, `042`, `044`, `048`, `051`, `053`, `057`, `058`, `059`, `061`, `064`, `073`, `078`, `082`, `090`, `099`, `103`, `105`, `106`, `111`, `119`, `120`, `122`, `123`, `128`.

Likely audience: customers choosing prescription masks, snorkellers upgrading gear, beginners unsure what to buy, and existing customers learning setup/care.

Primary orientation: equipment, underwater vision, snorkelling, some scuba-adjacent fit concepts.

### 3. Safety & Conditions

What belongs here: health checks, pre-snorkel planning, beach flags, buddy communication, water movement, extra safety recommendations, and surface hazards.

Why it exists: safety is the strongest trust-building thread in the recovered guide. It deserves a clear top-level home and should also be linked from every relevant skill/equipment lesson.

Existing material feeding it: `006`, `007`, `011`, `015`, `027`, `028`, `029`, `033`, `038`, `039`, `055`, `056`, `062`, `063`, `066`, `067`, `070`, `075`, `077`, `086`, `090`, `092`, `099`, `101`, `104`, `125`.

Likely audience: beginners, parents/families, visitors to unfamiliar beaches, less confident swimmers, and people planning a self-guided snorkel.

Primary orientation: snorkelling, safety, marine conditions.

### 4. In-Water Skills

What belongs here: entry and exit, mask clearing, snorkel clearing, duck diving, fin kicking, swimming technique, breathing control, floating, and treading water.

Why it exists: these are practical answer-focused lessons people may search for in the moment or learn in a guided sequence.

Existing material feeding it: `021` to `025`, `030`, `040`, `046`, `050`, `060`, `068`, `071`, `074`, `087`, `096`, `098`, `100`, `107`, `108`, `124`, `126`, `127`, `129`.

Likely audience: beginners, improving snorkellers, customers preparing before a trip, and people troubleshooting discomfort or confidence issues.

Primary orientation: snorkelling skills, water confidence, safety.

### 5. Ocean Science & Physiology

What belongs here: buoyancy, airspaces, light, sound, equalizing, heat transfer, breathing gases, hypocapnia, hypercapnia, shallow water blackout, and CO2-related risk.

Why it exists: this material gives the hub instructor depth and helps learners understand why safety guidance matters.

Existing material feeding it: `017`, `018`, `019`, `035`, `041`, `047`, `049`, `054`, `085`, `089`, `093`, `094`, `112`, `113`, `114`, `115`, `118`, `121`.

Likely audience: curious learners, snorkellers considering deeper dives or duck diving, scuba-adjacent readers, and learners who want reasons behind rules.

Primary orientation: both snorkelling and scuba-adjacent concepts, physiology, safety, underwater science.

### 6. Marine Life & Conservation

What belongs here: fish and marine life, coral, marine mammals, invertebrates, conservation, responsible practices, and ways to help the environment.

Why it exists: this supports enjoyable underwater experiences and gives Oceans Optics a values-led educational voice beyond equipment.

Existing material feeding it: `014`, `016`, `045`, `052`, `065`, `076`, `080`, `083`, `091`, `097`, `116`.

Likely audience: beginners, families, eco-conscious shoppers, tourists, and learners who want to understand what they see underwater.

Primary orientation: marine awareness, conservation, snorkelling, diving-adjacent environmental literacy.

## Categories Not Recommended As Top-Level Navigation

- "Academics": too abstract for customers and learners. Its contents should be redistributed into science, physiology, conditions, and marine life.
- "Extra Tips & Resources": too broad. Its contents are useful, but each item belongs in a more direct user-intent category.
- "Open Water": useful as a pathway label, but too ambiguous as a top-level hub category.
- "Products": product links should appear naturally inside gear and vision lessons rather than becoming a primary educational category at this stage.
