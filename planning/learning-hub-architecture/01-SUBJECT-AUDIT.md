## Main Overlap Notes

- Equipment setup and in-water skill content overlap most strongly around masks and snorkels. The proposed IA separates "choose/setup" from "use/clear underwater".
- Safety content is currently spread across introduction, checklist, beach flags, physiology, first aid, and open-water skills. The new hub should make safety findable from several paths.
- Marine life and conservation are related but have different learner intent: one answers "what am I seeing?" and the other answers "how should I behave around it?"
- Swimming technique, breathing, blackout risk, and first aid all touch safety. They should cross-link heavily but not be merged into one giant lesson.
