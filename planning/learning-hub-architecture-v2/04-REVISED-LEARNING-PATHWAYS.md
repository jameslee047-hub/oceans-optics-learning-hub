# Revised Learning Pathways

Pathways are guided routes through lessons. They do not duplicate lessons or create permanent categories.

## Start Here

Purpose: A short beginner onboarding path. The previous 12-lesson path was too long; this version focuses on approximately six core lessons plus optional Continue Learning.

Core lessons:

1. `R07 Health, Readiness and Personal Responsibility`
   - Why it belongs: readiness comes before gear, conditions, or skills.
2. `R08 Golden Rules for Safer Snorkelling`
   - Why it belongs: gives the learner core safety principles immediately.
3. `R01 Choosing a Mask`
   - Why it belongs: essential gear and Oceans Optics expertise start with mask/vision basics.
4. `R10 Pre-Snorkel Checklist and Planning`
   - Why it belongs: turns readiness into practical before-entry action.
5. `R14 Buddy Communication and Awareness`
   - Why it belongs: buddy behaviour is universal and foundational for a beginner.
6. `R17 Entry and Exit Techniques`
   - Why it belongs: provides a first basic in-water skill without overloading the path.

Continue Learning:

- `R11 Beach Flags and Surface Warnings`
- `R18 Clearing Your Snorkel`
- `R20 Mask Clearing Techniques`
- `R09 Surface Safety Devices`
- `R12 Currents & Rip Currents`

Notes: Beach flags vary by country and region, so `R11` is better treated as a reference lesson after the universal beginner behaviours are covered.

## Safer Snorkelling

Purpose: A safety-first route that avoids overloading learners with science unless directly relevant to risk decisions.

Lessons:

1. `R07 Health, Readiness and Personal Responsibility`
2. `R08 Golden Rules for Safer Snorkelling`
3. `R10 Pre-Snorkel Checklist and Planning`
4. `R11 Beach Flags and Surface Warnings`
5. `R12 Currents & Rip Currents`
6. `R13 Waves, Tides & Surge`
7. `R14 Buddy Communication and Awareness`
8. `R15 Breath-Hold Safety, CO2 & Shallow-Water Blackout`
9. `R16 Snorkelling Emergencies & First Aid Awareness`

Notes: `R15` and `R16` are high-stakes and require expert/current-guidance review before publication.

## Equipment Essentials

Purpose: Help learners choose and prepare core gear without mixing in unrelated practical skills.

Lessons:

1. `R01 Choosing a Mask`
2. `R02 Mask Fit, Positioning & Adjustment`
3. `R03 Mask Preparation, Defogging & Care`
4. `R04 Choosing and Setting Up a Snorkel`
5. `R05 Choosing Fins`
6. `R06 Exposure Protection & Staying Warm`
7. `R09 Surface Safety Devices`

Notes: Mask Clearing Techniques was removed from this pathway because it is an in-water troubleshooting skill, not an equipment-selection lesson.

## In-Water Skills Builder

Purpose: Give learners a practical progression for confidence and efficiency in the water.

Lessons:

1. `R17 Entry and Exit Techniques`
2. `R18 Clearing Your Snorkel`
3. `R20 Mask Clearing Techniques`
4. `R21 Efficient Kicking Techniques`
5. `R22 Relaxed Breathing & Efficient Movement`
6. `R23 Floating & Treading Water`
7. `R19 Duck Diving Basics`

Supporting science only where needed:

- `R26 Equalizing Basics` should be linked from duck diving.
- `R15 Breath-Hold Safety, CO2 & Shallow-Water Blackout` should be linked from duck diving and breathing/movement.

## Ocean-Aware Snorkeller

Purpose: Connect exploration, conditions, marine life, coral, and responsible behaviour.

Lessons:

1. `R28 Types of Snorkelling Experiences`
2. `R12 Currents & Rip Currents`
3. `R13 Waves, Tides & Surge`
4. `R29 Marine Life Basics`
5. `R30 Coral, Conservation and Responsible Behaviour`

Optional support:

- `R25 Light, Vision & Sound Underwater`
