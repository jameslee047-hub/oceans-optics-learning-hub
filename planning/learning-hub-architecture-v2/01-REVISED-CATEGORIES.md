# Revised Categories

This v2 architecture uses five permanent subject categories. `Start Here` is no longer a category; it becomes an onboarding pathway that pulls from lessons across the permanent categories.

## Category 1: Gear, Masks & Vision

Purpose: Help learners choose, fit, prepare, use, and care for the equipment that affects comfort, visibility, and confidence in the water.

What belongs here:

- Masks.
- Mask fit, positioning, adjustment, comfort checks, and care.
- Prescription-mask-adjacent source material where supported by the recovered guide.
- Underwater vision where supported by recovered light/vision material.
- Snorkels.
- Fins.
- Exposure equipment where it functions as gear selection and preparation.
- Equipment setup and aftercare.

Why it exists: this category should carry Oceans Optics' strongest commercial and specialist authority, especially around masks, underwater vision, prescription masks, and fitting expertise. The recovered guide supports a strong foundation, but dedicated prescription-mask lessons should be treated as future expansion until source material exists.

Recovered material feeding it:

- `009 Masks`
- `010 Snorkels`
- `011 Safety Devices` as safety-equipment cross-linking
- `012 Fins`
- `021 Pre Snorkel Routine` for exposure-related preparation
- `034`, `036`, `042`, `044`, `051`, `053`, `057`, `058`, `059`, `061`, `064`, `069`, `073`, `078`, `082`, `090`, `099`, `103`, `105`, `106`, `111`, `117`, `119`, `120`, `122`, `123`, `128`

Primary audience: prospective mask customers, existing Oceans Optics customers, beginner snorkellers, prescription-mask buyers, and people troubleshooting fit/fog/leak issues.

## Category 2: Safety & Conditions

Purpose: Make risk-awareness, readiness, planning, conditions, buddy responsibility, breath-hold risk, and emergency awareness easy to find.

What belongs here:

- Health readiness and personal responsibility.
- Golden rules.
- Safety devices.
- Pre-snorkel planning and checklists.
- Beach flags and surface warnings.
- Currents, rip currents, waves, tides, and surge.
- Buddy communication.
- Breath-hold safety and shallow-water blackout awareness.
- First-aid and emergency awareness.

Why it exists: safety should be visible, practical, and easy to scan. This category should not bury safety-critical content inside academic science pages.

Recovered material feeding it:

Primary audience: all learners, especially first-time snorkellers, families, cautious swimmers, travellers visiting unfamiliar water, and people planning unguided snorkels.

## Category 3: In-Water Skills

Purpose: Teach practical skills as focused, answer-sized lessons rather than grouping unrelated skills simply because they shared a Genially screen.

What belongs here:

- Entry and exit.
- Snorkel clearing.
- Duck diving.
- Mask clearing.
- Kicking styles.
- Relaxed breathing and efficient movement.
- Floating and treading water.

Why it exists: learners often search for one practical answer. This category should be scannable, mobile-friendly, and strongly linked to safety where a skill has risk implications.

Recovered material feeding it:

- `022`, `023`, `024`, `025`, `030`, `040`, `046`, `050`, `060`, `071`, `074`, `087`, `096`, `098`, `100`, `107`, `108`, `124`, `126`, `127`, `129`

Primary audience: beginners, improving snorkellers, existing customers preparing for use, and people troubleshooting confidence issues.

## Category 4: Underwater Science & Your Body

Purpose: Explain how the underwater environment and the human body behave in accessible, learner-focused terms.

What belongs here:

- Pressure.
- Buoyancy.
- Airspaces.
- Light, sound, and vision underwater.
- Equalising.
- Body temperature and heat transfer.
- CO2, breath-hold physiology, and shallow-water-blackout-adjacent concepts where science supports safety.

Why it exists: the recovered guide contains useful concepts, but "academics" is not a learner-friendly category. This category should answer "why does this happen to me underwater?"

Recovered material feeding it:

- `017`, `018`, `019`, `035`, `041`, `047`, `049`, `054`, `085`, `089`, `093`, `094`, `106`, `112`, `113`, `114`, `115`, `118`, `121`

Primary audience: curious learners, snorkellers starting to duck dive, scuba-adjacent readers, and people who want the reasons behind rules and techniques.

## Category 5: Marine Life & Conservation

Purpose: Support enjoyment, exploration, identification, respect for marine life, and responsible behaviour.

What belongs here:

- Types of snorkelling experiences and underwater environments.
- Marine life basics.
- Coral.
- Conservation.
- Responsible underwater behaviour.

Why it exists: this category helps the hub feel less transactional and more like an experienced guide to enjoyable underwater experiences.

Recovered material feeding it:

- `014`, `016`, `031`, `037`, `043`, `045`, `052`, `065`, `072`, `076`, `080`, `081`, `083`, `091`, `097`, `102`, `116`

Primary audience: beginners, families, eco-conscious customers, trip planners, and learners motivated by exploration.

## Not Permanent Categories

- `Start Here`: onboarding pathway, not a category.
- `Academics`: replaced by learner-friendly categories.
- `Open Water`: useful as a pathway/theme, but too broad as permanent navigation.
- `Extra Tips & Resources`: redistributed into clearer user-intent categories.
- `Products`: future product links should be embedded naturally in relevant Gear, Masks & Vision lessons.
