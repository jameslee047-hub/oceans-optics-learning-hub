# Curriculum Gap Audit

Companion to `content-development/LESSON-PLAN-OVERVIEW.md`. Reviews R01–R30 plus proposed R31 for duplicated subjects, misleading titles, scope-too-narrow/too-broad lessons, unhomed learner questions, underrepresented Oceans Optics specialist knowledge, and intro-vs-depth splits. R01–R30 are not renumbered; R31 is a proposal only.

Classifications used: `GOOD AS IS`, `SCOPE TOO NARROW`, `SCOPE TOO BROAD`, `OVERLAP`, `CONTENT GAP`, `TITLE MAY NEED REVIEW`, `NEW LESSON WARRANTED`.

## Cross-Cutting Observations

**Underrepresented Oceans Optics specialist knowledge:** prescription-mask/underwater-vision expertise is the clearest case (addressed above via R31). No other category shows a comparable gap between "what Oceans Optics likely knows as a specialist retailer" and "what the curriculum currently plans to teach" — the rest of the gaps found are process gaps (unassigned reviewers, unresolved scope decisions) rather than missing-expertise gaps.

**Orphaned lessons (no pathway membership):** R24 (Buoyancy, Pressure & Airspaces) and R27 (Why You Get Cold in Water) are both well-scoped but absent from all five current pathways. Neither is a scope problem — both are a discoverability problem. Recommend a pathway decision before launch rather than after, since adding a lesson to a pathway later is low-risk but a learner-facing dead end at launch is not.

**Category/pathway mismatch:** R09 (Surface Safety Devices) is categorized under Safety & Conditions but pathway-placed only under Equipment Essentials, not Safer Snorkeling. Worth a deliberate decision rather than an accidental omission.

**Intentional overlaps that need clear framing, not merging:** R06/R27 (exposure gear vs. heat-transfer physiology) and R12/R13 (tidal currents vs. tides-as-sea-level) are both correct splits in the architecture, but both risk reading as duplicated content to a learner unless each lesson explicitly states what the other one covers. Recommend this as a drafting-standard note (an explicit one-line "this lesson does not cover X — see [other lesson]" pattern), not a scope change.

**No other duplicated subjects or misleading titles found** among R02–R07, R09–R11, R13–R30 beyond what is listed above — the v2 architecture's SPLIT/KEEP decisions (documented per-lesson in `planning/learning-hub-architecture-v2/02-REVISED-LESSON-MAP.md`) already resolved most of the obvious overlap risk (e.g., mask selection vs. fit vs. care; snorkel clearing vs. duck diving; buoyancy/light/sound split from one v1 lesson into R24/R25).
