## Part 2 — Lesson-by-Lesson Review

### R01 — Choosing a Mask

**Purpose:** Help a first-time buyer make the mask decisions safe to make before fitting or prescription depth — lens format, skirt color, and intended use — while clearly naming fit and prescription need as separate, linked decisions.

**Learner should leave knowing:**
- What to consider before comparing mask styles (intended use, vision need, conditions)
- The difference between single-lens and dual-lens masks
- The difference between clear and blackout/dark skirts, by lighting condition
- That fit/seal and prescription selection are real, separate next steps — and where to go for each
- A practical shortlist order for narrowing down a mask

**Core coverage:** intended-use framing; single-lens vs. dual-lens; clear vs. blackout/dark skirt; naming (not teaching) vision-correction need and fit; a five-step shortlist; a "where to learn more" close.

**Covered elsewhere:**
- Detailed fit, seal, donning, adjustment → R02
- Defogging, cleaning, aftercare → R03
- SPH/CYL, nearsighted/farsighted, lens strength → R31
- Why vision behaves differently underwater → R25

**Scope decision:** EXPAND

**Reason:** Current draft under-delivers on its title — 5 of 10 evaluated buyer-decision topics are absent or only named in passing. Already agreed with James; carried forward here for completeness.

### R31 — Prescription Masks: Nearsightedness, Farsightedness & Lens Selection

**Purpose:** Give a snorkeler who needs vision correction a beginner-safe understanding of what a prescription mask is, whether they need one, how nearsighted/farsighted lens strength is chosen for off-the-shelf lenses, and which Oceans Optics tool to use next.

**Learner should leave knowing:**
- What a prescription mask is and whether they need one
- The difference between nearsighted and farsighted, and how each appears on a prescription
- What SPH and CYL mean, and that AXIS and ADD are not addressed by today's off-the-shelf mask lenses
- That each eye can have a different lens power, and today's mixed-type (nearsighted + farsighted) limitation
- The difference between the Lens Calculator (prescription strength) and the Mask Sizing Tool (physical fit)
- When to consult an optometrist instead of relying on this lesson

**Core coverage:** see `R01-R31-SCOPE-APPROVAL.md` for the full ten-heading structure; not repeated here to avoid duplicate-maintenance drift.

**Covered elsewhere:** Physical fit → R02; underwater-vision science/magnification → R25; general mask selection → R01.

**Scope decision:** NEW (approved as launch-level)

**Reason:** No existing or planned lesson teaches prescription-mask mechanics; real Oceans Optics tooling/data exists to support it, pending the factual-verification items already logged. **Approved for the Equipment Essentials pathway, where R31 may be identified as particularly relevant to learners who need vision correction** — see Part 6.
