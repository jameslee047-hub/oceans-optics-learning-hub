## CRITICAL — DO NOT PUBLISH WITHOUT REGIONAL FACT-CHECK
Flag colors and their meanings are NOT globally standardized. The specific color-to-meaning pairs in the legacy source (yellow = medium hazard, red = high hazard, double red = closed, purple = marine pests, red-over-yellow = lifeguard-supervised swimming area, black-and-white = watercraft area, yellow-and-black = watercraft prohibited, orange windsock = offshore wind/no inflatables, red-and-white = emergency evacuation) roughly match common conventions used in some regions (e.g., US-style beach warning flags), but this has NOT been independently verified against a current, authoritative source, and flag systems vary meaningfully by country and even by beach authority.

This draft presents the legacy list as "common conventions used in many places" throughout, with repeated, explicit instruction to confirm locally before relying on any flag. Do not remove or soften this framing without a completed regional fact-check.
