## Scope Note
This lesson covers non-current water movement: waves, tides (sea-level change), and surge. It explicitly does NOT cover currents, longshore currents, tidal currents, or rip currents — those stay in [Currents & Rip Currents](lesson:currents-and-rip-currents). The body repeats this boundary once, plainly, near the top, matching the approved split rationale.

## Review Notes
