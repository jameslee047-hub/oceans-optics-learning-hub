## Review Notes
- The legacy source's phrase "extend your breathhold" was deliberately NOT carried into this draft. It conflicts with [Breath-Hold Safety, CO2 & Shallow-Water Blackout](lesson:breath-hold-safety-co2-and-shallow-water-blackout) (R15), which explicitly instructs against encouraging longer breath-holds. This draft describes calm, efficient technique instead.
- The legacy source states snorkelers should remove the snorkel from their mouth during the dive and replace it on resurfacing. This is presented here as one common option rather than a firm instruction, since it isn't confirmed as the universally taught method (standard practice in some instruction keeps the snorkel in and clears it on resurfacing instead). Flagged for confirmation against current Oceans Optics-approved instruction.
- Per FINAL-CURRICULUM-APPROVAL, the Knowledge Check for this lesson should not be treated as final until it receives the same safety review as the R15 breath-hold content it connects to, given the direct breath-hold-risk link.
