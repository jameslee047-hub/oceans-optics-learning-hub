## CRITICAL — AWARENESS ONLY, NOT PROCEDURAL FIRST AID/CPR INSTRUCTION
This lesson deliberately does NOT reproduce CPR steps, chest-compression instructions, rescue-breath technique, or jellyfish-sting treatment steps from the legacy source, even though that material exists in `extracted-content/pages/110-cardiopulmonary-resuscitation-cpr.md`, `084-rescue-breaths.md`, and `109-jellyfish-stings.md`. Per the approved scope decision, this content stays at recognition/preparedness/getting-help level, and explicitly directs learners toward formal first-aid/CPR training rather than presenting itself as a substitute. CG-050 and CG-051 remain open safety-review items for any future decision to add procedural content; this draft does not attempt to resolve them, it works around them by staying at awareness level.
