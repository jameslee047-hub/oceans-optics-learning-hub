# R03 — Mask Preparation, Defogging & Care

## Curriculum Status
Category: Gear, Masks & Vision
Lesson type: Skill lesson
Pathways: Equipment Essentials
Curriculum decision: KEEP
Related lessons: R01, R02, R20

## Lesson Purpose
Cover new-mask preparation, defogging, cleaning, and aftercare so a mask performs well and lasts.

## Learner Outcomes
After this lesson the learner should be able to / understand:
- Understand why new masks fog and need treatment before first use
- Defog a mask using a repeatable method
- Clean a mask after each use
- Store a mask correctly to prevent mold and material damage

## Scope Boundary

### This lesson teaches
- Defogging method
- Post-use cleaning
- Storage and mold prevention

### This lesson introduces but does not teach in depth

### This lesson does NOT cover

### 2. Defogging a New or Used Mask

Purpose: Give a clear, repeatable defogging method.

Teaching points:
- Legacy method: apply white toothpaste to the inside lens, massage, rinse; repeat 2–3 times; leave the coated mask overnight; wash off with dish soap

Source basis:
- LEGACY OCEANS OPTICS SOURCE (`extracted-content/pages/061-no-more-fog.md`) — **flagged for current-practice check.** Toothpaste-based defogging is a widely known home method, but is debated among some mask/lens manufacturers as mildly abrasive and potentially harmful to certain lens coatings. Do not carry this forward as established best practice without confirmation.

Instructor/expert input:
- **Required.** Confirm the current recommended defogging method, and specifically whether it should differ for prescription/coated lenses (cross-link to R31).

Visual opportunity:
- Short step photo sequence — OPTIONAL.

### 3. Cleaning After Each Use

Purpose: Basic post-use cleaning routine.

Teaching points:
- Rinse thoroughly with fresh water
- Scrub gently with non-abrasive dish soap and a toothbrush if needed
- Dry completely before storing; avoid direct sunlight

Source basis:
- LEGACY OCEANS OPTICS SOURCE (`extracted-content/pages/119-after-care.md`)

Instructor/expert input:
- None required; standard, low-risk care advice.

Visual opportunity:
- NONE required.

### 4. Long-Term Storage and Mold Prevention

Purpose: Prevent mold and material degradation between uses.

Teaching points:
- Ensure fully dry before storing
- If mold appears, soak in white vinegar and scrub with a soft brush

- LEGACY OCEANS OPTICS SOURCE (`extracted-content/pages/119-after-care.md`)

## Key Takeaways
- New masks usually need defogging before first use
- Rinse and dry after every use
- Avoid direct sunlight when drying/storing
- White vinegar is the recovered mold-removal method
- Prescription lenses may need different defogging care — confirm before publishing (cross-link to R31)

## Instructor Tips Opportunities
- Current Oceans Optics-recommended defogging product/method, if different from the legacy toothpaste method
- Any prescription-lens-specific care guidance (backlog CG-030 — currently future/parked, no source material; do not invent)

## Common Mistakes Opportunities
- Skipping defogging on a brand-new mask and being surprised it fogs immediately
- Storing a mask while still damp
- Using abrasive cleaners or scrubbing too hard
- Drying in direct sunlight, degrading the skirt material

## Safety Notes
NONE. This is a care/maintenance lesson, not a safety lesson.

## Knowledge Check Plan

What understanding should be tested:
- That a new mask typically needs defogging before use
- Correct basic cleaning/drying routine
- Mold-prevention/storage habit

Proposed question concepts:
1. What should you do to a brand-new mask before your first snorkel? — catches the misconception that new masks are ready to use immediately.
2. How should a mask be dried before storage? — catches the misconception that direct sunlight is a fine or faster way to dry gear.
3. What should you do if you find mold on a mask? — catches the misconception that mold means the mask is ruined.
