## R31 — Prescription Masks: Nearsighted, Farsighted & Lens Selection

### 1. Proposed title

Prescription Masks: Nearsighted, Farsighted & Lens Selection

### 2. Category

Gear, Masks & Vision

### 3. Learner purpose

Give a snorkeler who needs vision correction a beginner-safe understanding of what a prescription mask is, whether they need one, how nearsighted/farsighted lens strength is chosen for off-the-shelf mask lenses, and which Oceans Optics tool to use next — without positioning this lesson as a substitute for an eye exam.

### 4. Proposed section headings, with the exact learner questions each answers

**1. What Is a Prescription Mask, and Do I Need One?**
- What is a prescription (corrective) mask, in plain terms?
- How do I know if I actually need one, versus a standard mask?

**2. Nearsighted vs. Farsighted, in Plain Terms**
- What's the difference between nearsighted (myopia) and farsighted (hyperopia)?
- How does each show up on a normal eyeglass prescription?

**3. Reading Your Prescription: SPH, CYL, AXIS, and ADD**
- What does SPH mean, and what does a negative vs. positive number mean?
- What does CYL mean?
- What is AXIS, and does it matter for a mask lens?
- What is ADD, and does it matter for a mask lens?

**4. What a Standard Mask Lens Can and Cannot Correct**
- Are mask lenses sphere-only, or can they correct astigmatism directly?
- What happens to my CYL/AXIS if the lens only corrects SPH?
- Does lens-strength availability vary by mask model?

**5. Different Prescriptions in Each Eye**
- Can each eye have a different lens strength?
- What if I only need correction in one eye (plano in the other)?
- What if one eye is nearsighted and the other is farsighted?

**6. Choosing Your Lens Strength**
- How do I turn my prescription into a lens-strength selection?
- When should I use the Lens Calculator?

**7. Fit and Prescription Are Two Separate Decisions**
- Does getting the right prescription mean the mask will fit my face?
- When should I use the Mask Sizing Tool instead of the Lens Calculator?

**8. Snorkeling vs. Scuba, Where It Matters**
- Does anything about prescription-lens selection change between snorkeling and scuba use? *(include only if a real, approved difference exists — otherwise state plainly that it doesn't, rather than omit the question)*

**9. When to Get Professional Optical Advice**
- When should I talk to an optometrist instead of relying on this lesson or the calculator?

**10. Where to Learn More**
- Links to Lens Calculator, Mask Sizing Tool, R01, R02, R25.

## Factual Verification

Per source-priority guidance: prescription-mask material in this repository spans several years, and Oceans Optics has since updated Lens Calculator logic, prescription handling, product/lens ranges, mask-fit tooling, product availability, and internal guidance. Older guide/theme copy is **not** treated as automatically current or authoritative. Each row below is tagged:

- **CURRENT** — sourced from what appears to be actively-maintained operational tooling/data (the Lens Calculator, Mask Sizing Tool, and mask/lens database files carry recent asset timestamps and, in `maskData.js`, explicit `// updated` comments — used here as evidence of active maintenance, not as proof of a specific update date).
- **LEGACY — REVIEW BEFORE USE** — sourced from older guide-style or educational-page copy (per James: content in this style predates the current tooling and is not assumed current). Useful for identifying topics; not to be reused as fact without a current-source check.
- **VERIFY EXTERNALLY** — the claim is an optics/medical/vision-correction statement (not just an Oceans Optics product-capability statement) and must be checked against external optometric standards, not just against an internal source. A row can carry both a source tag and this tag.

Where a current source and a legacy source disagree, or where a claim is scientific/medical, this document does not attempt to resolve it — it is flagged only.

## Relationship Between Lessons

As proposed by James:

```
R01 Choosing a Mask
  → R02 Fit
  → R31 Prescription Masks (when vision correction is needed)
  → R25 for deeper underwater-vision science
```

**Suggested better order:** put R31 before R02 on the prescription branch, and treat R25 as an optional side-link rather than a required last step — because lens strength/model availability can constrain *which* mask is even eligible for fitting, so a prescription customer benefits from resolving "which model" (R31) before "how do I fit and adjust it" (R02). R25 answers "why does my vision behave this way underwater," which is useful context but not a required step in the buying decision, so it fits better as an optional link from both R01 (question 2) and R31 (question 3) than as a fourth sequential step.

```
R01 Choosing a Mask
  ├─ no vision correction needed → R02 Fit
  └─ vision correction needed → R31 Prescription Masks → R02 Fit (for the selected model)

R25 Light, Vision & Sound Underwater — optional link from R01 and R31, not a required step
```
