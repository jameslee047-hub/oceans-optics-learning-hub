Human-editable master backlog for Learning Hub content gaps, future-content ideas, instructor input needs, research needs, and safety/current-guidance review requirements.

## Classification Rules

A content gap does not automatically become a new lesson.

Allowed types:

- EXISTING LESSON ENHANCEMENT
- FUTURE LESSON CANDIDATE
- FUTURE PATHWAY / FEATURE
- INSTRUCTOR INPUT
- FACT CHECK / RESEARCH
- SAFETY / CURRENT GUIDANCE REVIEW

Approved learner-facing categories:

- Gear, Masks & Vision
- Safety & Conditions
- In-Water Skills
- Underwater Science & Your Body
- Marine Life & Conservation

Use `CATEGORY DECISION REQUIRED` only when the item is genuinely learner-facing but does not yet have a safe category decision.
